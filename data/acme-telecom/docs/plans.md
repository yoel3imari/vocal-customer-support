# NovaCell Plans

## Smart Light — $19/month
- 5 GB of 5G data, unlimited talk & text.
- Speeds reduced to 1 Mbps after the monthly data cap.
- 1-year contract; early cancellation fee is $60.

## Smart Plus — $29/month
- 25 GB of 5G data, unlimited talk & text, free weekend data in the EU.
- Speeds reduced to 1 Mbps after the cap.
- 1-year contract; early cancellation fee is $60.

## Ultra Unlimited — $45/month
- Unlimited 5G data (fair use: after 100 GB per month speeds may drop during peak hours).
- Unlimited talk & text, 15 GB hotspot per month.
- No contract — cancel any month with 30 days' notice.

## Add-ons
- Extra 5 GB data top-up: $8, valid for the rest of the current billing cycle.
- International calling pack: $12/month for 300 minutes to the US, Canada and Mexico.
- Device protection (accidental damage + theft): $9/month.

## Changing plan
- You can move to a higher plan at any time; it takes effect immediately and the price
  difference is prorated on the next bill.
- Moving to a lower plan takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
- Plan changes can be made in the NovaCell app or by calling support.
