# NovaCell Network Outages & Faults

## Checking for an outage
- The live network status map is at status.novacell.example (support agents can check the
  status board while on the call).
- Support can see per-region incidents: reported outage, estimated fix time, and affected
  services (voice, data, SMS).

## Standard outage guidance
- If there is a known outage in the caller's area, tell them the estimated fix time and
  that no compensation is needed — service resumes automatically.
- Outages longer than 24 hours automatically qualify for a one-day credit, applied on the
  next bill without the customer asking.
- Do not book a technician visit if the area has a known outage.

## No service, no known outage
1. Ask the caller to toggle airplane mode on and off.
2. Ask them to restart the phone.
3. Check the SIM is seated correctly if they can.
4. Ask for their postcode and check the coverage map for planned maintenance.
- If none of that helps, open a technical ticket and offer to transfer to Technical Support.

## Coverage
- 5G coverage: city centres and major towns. 4G: 99% of the population.
- Rural or basement locations may drop to 3G or no signal; a signal booster is available
  for $79 for customers in verified weak-signal areas.

## Lost or stolen phone
- Report the loss and the line can be suspended immediately (free), and a replacement
  SIM is $10 including shipping.
- Suspension is reversible within 30 days at no cost.
