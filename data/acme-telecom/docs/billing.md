# NovaCell Billing

## Billing cycle
- Bills are generated on the 1st of each month and charged automatically to the saved
  payment method three days later.
- The current bill and the previous 24 bills are available in the NovaCell app and by
  calling support.

## Common charges
- First invoice includes a $10 one-off SIM activation fee.
- Late payment fee: $7, applied if the bill is not paid within 10 days of the due date.
- Line suspension for non-payment happens 15 days after the due date; a $15 reconnect
  fee applies to restore the line.
- Part-month charges are prorated: if you changed plans mid-month, both plans appear as
  prorated lines on the next bill.

## Payments
- Accepted: credit/debit card, SEPA direct debit, PayPal.
- Payment can be made in the app, on the website, or over the phone with a card.
- Support agents never ask for full card numbers, PINs or passwords.

## Disputes and refunds
- Billing disputes are handled by the Billing team; support agents can open a dispute
  ticket and transfer the call.
- Disputed amounts over $20 are put on hold while the ticket is open — the line stays
  active until the dispute is resolved.
- Refunds are approved case by case and are issued to the original payment method within
  5 to 10 business days.
- Double-charge claims are verified against the payment history; if confirmed, the
  duplicate is refunded in full.
