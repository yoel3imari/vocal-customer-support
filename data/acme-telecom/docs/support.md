# NovaCell Support Hours & Handoffs

## Hours
- Phone support: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- Emergency line (outages and lost/stolen phones): 24/7.

## Handoff teams
- Billing team: disputes, refunds, contract cancellations, payment plans. Mon-Fri 9:00-18:00.
- Technical support: faults that survive the standard checks, SIM or device issues.
- Retention: customers who want to cancel and might be open to an offer.

## How to hand off
- If the caller asks for a human, transfer gracefully: "Of course — let me connect you
  to a colleague."
- Warm transfers only for billing disputes and technical faults; everything else can be
  a callback ticket.
- Callback tickets are answered within 4 working hours.
