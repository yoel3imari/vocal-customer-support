# ShopVerse Orders & Payments

## Order statuses
- "Processing": payment authorised, warehouse has not packed it yet.
- "Packed": boxed and waiting for the carrier; address changes are no longer possible.
- "Shipped": with the carrier; tracking is live.
- "Delivered": carrier marked it delivered.
- Backordered items show an estimated restock date on the product page.

## Cancellations
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge while "Processing".
- Once "Packed", the order cannot be cancelled; the customer should refuse delivery or
  return it after arrival.

## Payment methods
- Credit/debit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
- We do not accept cash on delivery, bank transfer or cryptocurrency.
- Payment is captured when the order ships, except for backorders where it is captured
  at checkout.

## Payment problems
- Declined card: the customer should check the billing address and try another method;
  a pending authorisation is released by the bank in 3 to 5 business days.
- Duplicate charge: verify in the order history, then transfer to the Payments team for a
  refund of the duplicate.
- Promo codes cannot be applied after an order is placed; support can cancel and re-place
  the order if it is still "Processing".

## Accounts
- Password resets are self-serve via the app or website.
- Support never asks for a password or a one-time code.
- Guest orders can be looked up with the order number plus the email used at checkout.
