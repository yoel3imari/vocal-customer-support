# ShopVerse Shipping

## Standard shipping
- Orders over $50 ship free. Orders under $50 cost $4.95.
- Standard delivery: 3 to 5 business days.
- Express delivery: $9.95, 1 to 2 business days if ordered before 2 pm.
- We ship within the continental US only. No international shipping yet.

## Tracking
- A tracking link is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse (usually within
  24 hours of ordering).
- Tracking can also be checked in the app under "My orders".
- If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, support can open a carrier trace.

## Delays and lost parcels
- If a parcel is more than 3 business days late, support can open a carrier trace.
- A parcel is declared lost 10 business days after the expected delivery date; at that
  point a free replacement or full refund is offered.
- Weather and holiday delays are posted on the shopverse.example status page.

## Address changes
- Address changes are possible until the order status changes to "packed" (usually
  within 2 hours of ordering). After that the parcel must be refused at delivery.
