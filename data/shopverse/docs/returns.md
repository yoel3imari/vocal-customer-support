# ShopVerse Returns & Refunds

## Return window
- Unopened items: 30 days from delivery for a full refund.
- Opened items: 14 days from delivery, provided all accessories and packaging are included.
- Defective items: 30 days for a replacement or refund, plus the manufacturer warranty
  after that.

## Non-returnable
- Gift cards, hygiene products, and custom-engraved items cannot be returned.
- Digital downloads are non-refundable once the licence key is shown.

## How returns work
1. Customer requests a return in the app or with support.
2. A prepaid return label is emailed within 1 business day.
3. Return shipping is free for defective or wrong items; otherwise $4.95 is deducted
   from the refund.
4. Refunds are issued within 3 business days of the warehouse receiving the parcel, to
   the original payment method; the bank may take another 5 to 10 business days to post it.

## Damaged or wrong item
- Ask for the order number and a short description.
- Offer a replacement or refund immediately if the item arrived damaged or wrong.
- No need to return high-value damaged items under $30; for anything above that, a
  prepaid label is issued.
- Escalate repeat cases (more than two in 12 months) to Customer Care.

## Exchanges
- Exchanges are handled as a return plus a new order. Support can waive shipping on the
  new order when the original item was defective.
